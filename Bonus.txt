Bonus Features Added (Ice Cream Sales Profits):
1. Live Summary Statistics & R² Bar:
   Displays total days loaded, average temperature, average profit, peak profit, and R² above the graph.
2.Profit Predictor:
   Lets the user enter any temperature to predict expected profit.
3. Temperature Unit Toggle (°C / °F):
   Allows switching between Celsius and Fahrenheit
4. Interactive Point Hover Tooltip:
   Hovering over any scatter point displays a floating tooltip showing that day's temperature and actual profit.
